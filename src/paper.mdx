  <p>HOIST learns to guide an externally suspended payload through whole-body motion and intermittent contact. We initialize a vision-language-action policy from 50 VR demonstrations, then refine placement and stopping with small batches of reinforcement-learning rollouts while keeping the policy and whole-body controller frozen. In simulation, 20 additional rollouts reduce mean position error from 22.44 to 2.54 cm. On a real humanoid, 30 rollouts reduce it from 9.28 to 6.38 cm. These experiments evaluate a controlled suspended-box task; construction footage illustrates its motivation.</p>
